Who We Are

Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) His Presence Newcastle is a vibrant and inclusive community church that values diversity and unity. Individuals from various backgrounds, cultures, and walks of life gather together to worship and grow in faith as one body.

We are committed to sharing our experience born out of encounters in God's presence, thereby creating an atmosphere and environment where everyone would have a genuine encounter with God and experience the fullness of God's presence in their lives.

As ambassadors of God's love, we sow seeds of grace and kindness, fostering a global community united by the shared experience of God's unconditional love bringing men unto salvation.

Declaration

By faith, I declare that my life, my family, and everything concerning me are entering a season of enlargement this year. The windows of heaven are open over me, pouring out blessings too great to contain. Through my obedience, I am blessed to be a blessing to my generation, graced with favor for exceptional results, and anointed to live a life of fruitfulness and impact.

My vision is enlarged, and I rise above every limitation that once held me back. My eyes are opened to the abundant opportunities and boundless possibilities that God has prepared for me in 2025. I think BIG, I dream God-sized dreams and partner with Him to see my generation, communities, and nations transformed as these dreams come to fruition.

This year, I refuse to settle for less, for I know that my God is not limited by what I can see or comprehend. I trust in His unlimited ability to exceed every of my expectation for God is able to do immeasurably more than I could ever dream or hope for. The Lord will increase me a thousand times more, just as He has promised. I am enlarged spiritually, physically, and materially, as I step into new territories where my prayers and actions will create a lasting legacy.

I operate in all-round dominion through the abundance of grace and the gift of righteousness. This year, sickness, lack, and every form of danger are forbidden in and around me, in Jesus' name.

Our children in HPN are taught of the Lord, set apart for His purpose and great shall be their peace. The excellent spirit of God rests upon all that concerns them. My home is not affected by the corruption, darkness, and perversion in the world. No weapon formed against me shall prosper.

I am led, directed and guided by the Holy Spirit this week. This new week ushers me into a life of victory, enlargement and dominion in Jesus' name.
